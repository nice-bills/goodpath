What the research says

The winning bar is not "uses G$ somewhere." It is meaningful protocol usage, live product, Celo distribution, and social growth.

GoodBuilders

Top two matters

The buildathon is tied to prizes and fast-track access into a funded 3-month program for the top two projects. That means the demo must look fundable, not just functional.

GoodDollar

Meaningful integration

GoodDollar developer docs call out meaningful new protocol integrations. The app should prove G$ identity, claim, payments, community support, savings, and streaming.

Celo

Celo is the chain story

G$ on Celo is the official token surface we should build around. Do not market multi-chain. Do not support Fuse in the MVP. Everything should say Celo mainnet.

Superfluid

Streaming is the deep edge

G$ is a native SuperToken on Celo, and GoodBuilders itself is connected to streaming/continuous funding. A stream quest is much stronger than another transfer quest.

Social login

Login must be boring-easy

Privy embedded wallets let email/Google users enter without seed phrases. This is critical because GoodDollar users are mobile-first and often new to crypto.

Contract

Upgradeable, but narrow

Use an OpenZeppelin UUPS proxy for the GoodPath contract. It records product events and season logic; it must not custody user G$ in v1.

Product concept

Replace "quest checklist" with a solo ladder whose score only comes from useful Celo actions. Social features add upside, not dependency.

01 Join with social login Email or Google creates/links an embedded Celo wallet. Wallet users can still connect directly.
02 Verify and claim GoodDollar identity + daily UBI claim. This is entry, not the whole product.
03 Move G$ with purpose Tip a public jar, support a pool, save, or stream. Friends are optional; useful G$ movement is not.
04 Deploy G$ Open a savings position or Superfluid G$ stream. This becomes the deep integration moment.
05 Climb your run Personal bests, weekly divisions, shareable receipts, Celoscan proof, and streak shields.

Upgradeable contract

The contract should make the project serious without creating unnecessary custody/security risk.

Contract Why it exists V1 methods/events Upgrade plan
GoodPathReceipt Public Celo mainnet proof that the app is not only SQLite points. recordQuest, recordReferral, joinSquad, recordSeasonScore; emits quest/referral/squad/season events. OpenZeppelin UUPS proxy, owner is a multisig/admin wallet, storage-gap reserved for v2.
GoodPathSeason later On-chain season config if GoodBuilders funding continues. Season windows, multipliers, allowlisted quest verifier, optional badge minting. V2 upgrade or separate contract after demo, not needed before mainnet launch.
Important: v1 should not hold user funds. Let GoodDollar token, savings SDK, and Superfluid handle money movement. Our contract records verified product proofs and makes the league composable.

Social, competitive, fun

These are not decorations. They are the growth loop and judging story.

Core loop

GoodPath League

Weekly ranking based on Celo proofs: claim streak, G$ tipped, G$ supported, stream opened, savings action, verified referral, and optional squad completion. The user sees "how to beat your previous run" before they see generic onboarding copy.

Fun

Streak shields

Earn one missed-day forgiveness by completing solo challenges: claim three days, support a pool, open a stream, or beat yesterday's score.

Social

Optional squads

3-5 people can pool activity into a weekly squad rank, but users never need friends to progress. Squads are a multiplier for growth, not the base game.

Shareable

Receipt cards

Receipts show rank, G$ moved, number of proofs, Celo links, and invite code. They should feel like a scorecard, not a certificate.

Solo-first design

The product must be satisfying with one user, one judge account, or one person with no friends in the app.

Default

Personal best ladder

Your first opponent is your previous run: fastest path, longest streak, most G$ moved, most proof types, first stream, and weekly score.

No friends needed

Public targets

Tip a rotating community jar, support a featured GoodCollective pool, save G$, or open a stream. The app always offers a useful destination.

Competition

Divisions, not giant leaderboards

Start users in tiny seeded divisions: Bronze, Silver, Gold. A user can be #2 of 8 even when the app only has a few early users.

Judge demo

Seeded season

For demos, show anonymized seeded runners plus the live user. The live user's Celo actions change rank in real time without pretending seeded rows are real users.

Social later

Rivals before squads

Instead of requiring a friend group, let a user pick one public rival or "beat the median" challenge. This creates competition with low liquidity.

Contract fit

Solo receipts first

The upgradeable contract records solo run events in v1. Squad/referral events are present but optional, so mainnet launch is not blocked by network effects.
