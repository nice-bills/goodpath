Stakeholder one-liner

G$ Path: social or MetaMask on Celo, GoodDollar-sponsored gas, verified claim, then compulsory G$ velocity — tip, support, or stream — ranked on-chain. We use G$ as a SuperToken and the official savings/engagement stack, not a side quest app.
